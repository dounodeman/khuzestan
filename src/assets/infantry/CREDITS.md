# Infantry photo sprites: sources and licences

Each soldier figure here is cut out of a photograph from the Iran-Iraq War on Wikimedia Commons
(see `tools/photo-sprites/infantry.py` and `tools/photo-sprites/configs/infantry.json`).

| Figures | Source photo | Author / source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `iran_stand_b25.png`, `iraq_stand_b25.png` | [Teenage soldiers (Basij volunteers) during the Iran-Iraq War (25).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Teenage_soldiers_(Basij_volunteers)_during_the_Iran-Iraq_War_(25).jpg) | Fars News Agency | Public domain (as marked on Commons) |
| `iran_prone_b36.png`, `iraq_prone_b36.png` | [Teenage soldiers (Basij volunteers) during the Iran-Iraq War (36).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Teenage_soldiers_(Basij_volunteers)_during_the_Iran-Iraq_War_(36).jpg) | Fars News Agency | Public domain (as marked on Commons) |

Changes made: soldier traced and cut out of the background, rotated so his head points the way
the squad faces (the game's top-down "forward"), scaled to about 48 px per metre. The `iraq_*`
figures are the same men re-toned to Iraqi khaki: freely licensed photos of armed Iraqi infantry
from the war could not be found.
