# Tank photo sprites: sources and licences

Each sprite here is cut out of a photograph from Wikimedia Commons, rectified to a plan view and
split into hull and turret layers (see `tools/photo-sprites/`). The photographers' licences carry
over to the derived sprites listed below.

| Unit | Files | Source photo | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-72 (`iq_t72`) | `iq_t72_hull.png`, `iq_t72_turret.png` | [T-72 Ryazan 2.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:T-72_Ryazan_2.jpg) | Max071086 | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |

Changes made to every photo: perspective-corrected to a top-down view, background removed, turret
and gun separated from the hull, the hull deck under the turret filled in, scaled to 40 px per metre.
The derived images are released under the same licence as their source photo.
